Hi Erich, long time no see.

This is Apple playing numbers games with their versions combined with gfortran thinking it can get the OS version by subtracting 9 from the kernel version.

In short: To set gfortran back on tracks, add the following to your configuration (using config.site <http://config.site/>, or whichever mechanism you prefer):

FFLAGS=-mmacosx-version-min=26.0

(AFAIR 27.0 also works.)

Also notice that Simon is pushing the development version towards using flang instead of gfortran. See his note from 2026-09-21 on this list.

> Dear all,
> 
> On an M4 Max with macOS 27.0.1 (26A434) and CRAN R 4.6.1, with R switched to the vecLib BLAS (libRblas.dylib -> libRblas.vecLib.dylib), matrix products are silently wrong whenever a signal that R handles arrives during the BLAS call. Two R-level triggers we hit:
> - the SIGCHLD handler that parallel installs: children of mcparallel()/mclapply() exiting while the parent multiplies;
> - Rprof()'s SIGPROF: R dies with an illegal instruction.
> 
> The fault is not in R. The C program below reproduces it with Accelerate's cblas_dgemm and an empty SIGUSR1 handler: 328 of 500 products of 400x400 matrices are wrong, some cells exactly zero. Inside the handler, SVCR shows that the thread is still in SME streaming mode with ZA enabled. Ignored signals, or signals blocked during the call, give 0 of 500 wrong. R's reference BLAS (libRblas.0.dylib) and OpenBLAS 0.3.30 (non-SME core) give 0 wrong under the same signal load.
> 
> The public xnu source (xnu-12377.121.6, the macOS 26 line) points to the cause: signal delivery does not leave streaming mode, and sigreturn truncates the streaming vector registers to 128 bits. If that reading is right, macOS 26 on M4 is affected too; we have not run the test on macOS 26. Reported to Apple as FB25068706.
> 
> This differs from the fork-safety problem discussed in January (https://stat.ethz.ch/pipermail/r-sig-mac/2026-January/015322.html). There the forked child fails. Here the parent's own product is corrupted, and no fork is needed: any handled signal does it.
> 
> Until Apple fixes it, users of SME Macs (M4 and later) who switched to vecLib may want to switch back to the reference BLAS, the CRAN default:
> 
>     cd /Library/Frameworks/R.framework/Resources/lib
>     ln -sf libRblas.0.dylib libRblas.dylib
> 
> A line in the vecLib section of the R for macOS FAQ might help others.
